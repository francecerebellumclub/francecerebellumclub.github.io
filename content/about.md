## Our Mission

The France Cerebellum Club is a scientific organization whose goal is to promote interactions and collaboration between scientists sharing a common interest in the study of the cerebellum.  This is mainly achieved through the organization of a yearly meeting - the *Cerebellum Days* - featuring a balanced combination of both prestigious international keynote lecturers and early-career cerebellar scientists (PhD students or postdocs), thereby promoting the careers of young researchers and the future of our scientific community. Importantly, we place particular emphasis on maintaining  a transdisciplinary spirit, by addressing cerebellar research through complementary modalities, ranging from developmental and molecular aspects to more integrative and computational approaches.
