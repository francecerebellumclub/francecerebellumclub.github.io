The France Cerebellum Club organizes the annual *Cerebellum Days* conference which brings together a broad network of researchers united by a common interest in the cerebellum. The scientific program spans a broad range of topics, covering Developmental and Evolutionary Perspectives, Synapses, Circuits and Connectivity, Neuronal Dynamics, From Circuits to Behavior, Computation, and the Cerebellum in Health and Disease. This meeting, which unites the French cerebellum community with leading international researchers, offers an ideal first opportunity for young scientists to present their work outside their home laboratory and meet leading scientists in the field. Its supportive yet scientifically rigorous environment allows young researchers to gain visibility, receive expert feedback, and integrate into the broader community.
